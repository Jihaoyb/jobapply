# Resume forms using approved answers

## Parent PRD

[Job application and outreach pilot](prd.md)

## What to build

Resolve fields by scoped exact matches, approved aliases and guarded Luna equivalence. Resume at the reviewed field.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Offer one-time, employer and global saves. Respect choices, polarity, time horizon and location.
- [ ] Calibrate on 30–50 handwritten pairs and evaluate on held-out examples. Ambiguity pauses despite a high score.
- [ ] Verified in 007: a ticket answer resumes the form at the paused field.
